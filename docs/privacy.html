Signature Maker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Signature Maker ("the app", on the App Store as Electronic Signature Creator) makes signature images on iPhone and places them on documents you already have. It is made by Brian Kershner and built so that your signatures and your documents stay on your device.

Your signatures

Signatures and initials you draw or type are saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone, as images with the strokes or the words they were made from. They are not uploaded, synced or sent to us or anyone else. There is no account and no server, and the app works offline.

Your documents

When you open a PDF or a photo to sign, from Files, from Photos or with "Open in" from another app, the app works on its own copy on your iPhone and never changes the original. The signed copy is saved on your iPhone, and the last 20 are listed in the app. They go only where you choose to send them (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop and so on).

Photos and the camera

Choosing a photo of a form uses Apple's photo picker, which hands the app only the photo you pick; the app cannot see the rest of your library. Save to Photos asks for permission to add images only, the first time you use it; the app never reads your library. The app does not use the camera, the microphone, your location or your contacts.

Purchases

If you buy Signature Maker Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app, never your name, your signatures or your documents. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder on your iPhone the day before the trial ends. It is created on the device. No push service is used and nothing is sent from a server.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect usage data. On the App Store the app is listed as Data Not Collected.

Deleting your data

Delete a signature from the Library, a signed copy from Documents, everything at once with Settings, Delete all signatures and documents, or delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.

What the app is not

Signature Maker creates a signature image and places it on your document. It does not verify identity or certify signatures, and it keeps no audit trail.
