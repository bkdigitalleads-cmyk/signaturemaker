Make a signature

On Create, draw on the paper with a finger or Apple Pencil. Pick a pen (Ink, Ballpoint, Marker, Fountain), an ink and a thickness first; undo, redo and clear are under the paper. For a long name, tap Wide and turn the phone. Or tap Type instead, type your name and pick one of twelve script fonts. Tap Save signature.

Export a transparent PNG

After you save, the export screen opens (or open any signature from the Library and tap Export). Transparent PNG is chosen; the checkerboard shows it is clear, and On a letter shows it in context. Pick 2x or 3x, then Share, Save to Photos or Copy. PNG and JPG on white are there too.

Sign a PDF

From Mail or Files: tap the attachment, tap Share, and choose Signature Maker. Or open the Documents tab and tap Open a PDF, or A photo for a picture of a form. Tap Signature to place your default signature, drag it onto the line, and pinch or pull its corner to size it. Initials, Date and Text work the same way. Tap Done signing: a new file named after the original with "signed" added is saved in Documents, and Send it back opens Mail or the share sheet. The original is never changed.

If Signature Maker is not in the share sheet, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn it on.

Pro, prices and restoring

Making and saving signatures is always free, and so are your first export and your first signed document. After that, an export or a signed document offers Pro; close it to get the file with a small "Made with Signature Maker" mark in the corner. Pro is $4.99 a week with 3 days free for new subscribers, $19.99 a year, or $39.99 once for lifetime, and adds clean files, every pen and font, initials and the date and text stamps. On a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app, your name, Subscriptions.

What it is

Signature Maker creates a signature image and places it on your document. It does not verify identity or certify signatures. If a document needs an identity-verified electronic signature, ask the sender which service they require.

Privacy

Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no uploads, no analytics. See the privacy policy.
