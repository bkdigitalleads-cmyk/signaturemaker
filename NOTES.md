  - How it works: src/strings/en.ts holds every word the app shows or reads out, by screen; de.ts, fr.ts, es.ts and it.ts have exactly its shape (typed `Strings`, so tsc fails on a missing or extra string). Where a number, a name or a price goes in, the entry is a small function, so each language puts it where its grammar wants it (plurals, German cases, French spacing). src/i18n.ts (pure) picks the language: the first of the phone's languages the app has, in the phone's order, else English; `t` is one object whose sections are swapped in place. src/format.ts (pure) formats dates and numbers with the phone's full locale tag (de-CH gets Swiss numbers; a Portuguese phone gets English words and Portuguese dates). src/locale.ts reads expo-localization once; index.ts loads it before App, so every screen starts in the right language. iOS restarts an app when its language changes.
  - New dependency, the one Brian approved: expo-localization ~57.0.2. `npx expo install` could not reach api.expo.dev from this session (the proxy refused it), so the version is the one Expo SDK 57 pins in node_modules/expo/bundledNativeModules.json, installed with npm; it brings rtl-detect with it. No other package.

Nov. 2026") and is drawn into the PDF as before.
